# Better Slack — Landing Copy

**COMM-026**

## Headline

Chat is for coordination. **Posts are for decisions.**

## Subhead

Forum-first team communication for engineering teams — where humans and AI agents collaborate with structure, not noise.

## Problem

- Critical decisions buried in Slack threads that die after 4 messages
- AI agents bolted on as bots with no permissions or audit trail
- 43% of developers say chat hurts focus
- 67% of eng managers re-litigate decisions because nobody can find them

## Solution

**Better Slack** replaces the firehose with a forum:

- **Thread-first channels** — see discussions, not message streams
- **Versioned Posts** — ADRs, incident reports, RFCs that persist
- **Agent governance** — capability-based permissions, audit logs, proposal flows
- **MCP-native** — agents integrate via standard protocol

## Positioning

For 20–100 person engineering teams using GitHub, Linear, and AI coding agents who need a system of record for technical discussion.
